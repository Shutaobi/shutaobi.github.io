\section*{Research Experience}
\textbf{Noncommutative Algebraic Geometry and Threefold Flops}\par
My research focuses on contraction algebras and their connections with threefold flops and Gopakumar--Vafa invariants. I have worked on the following topics.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Semisimple deformations of contraction algebras and the structure of their simple modules.
  \item Flatness and base change for contraction algebras, their centres, and their zeroth Hochschild homology.
  \item Geometric realization of completed Jacobi algebras as contraction algebras.
\end{itemize}
